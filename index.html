Our Vision & Mission

To be the leading information and communications technology (ICT) agency in public service delivery, rendering efficient, value-added ICT services to the public sector in a secure, cost-effective and integrated manner that contributes to citizen convenience.

About SITA

The Government's IT Business Partner

Established by the SITA Act of 1998, we consolidate and coordinate the State's technology resources, driving savings, capability and interoperability across South Africa.

01

Mandatory Services

Transversal ICT systems, network infrastructure and security operations mandated for all national and provincial departments.

02

Non-Mandatory Services

Extended ICT consulting, procurement facilitation and managed services available to any public sector entity.

03

Digital Transformation

Driving e-Government through innovative platforms that improve citizen experience and public service efficiency.
